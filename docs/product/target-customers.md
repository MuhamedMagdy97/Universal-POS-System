# Target Customers

## MVP focus
General retail stores (clothing, general goods) — simplest checkout, tax,
and compliance path. Fastest route to a working MVP.

## Selector presets (all present in onboarding, only Retail fully works in MVP)
- General retail — fully works in MVP.
- Grocery / supermarket — preset (kg units, expiry flag); scale + waste deferred.
- Fruit & vegetable market — same as grocery preset.
- Bakery / butcher — grocery preset; weighted-item integration deferred.
- Restaurant / café — preset (dine-in/takeaway type, service charge); KDS, table map, recipes, modifiers/combos deferred.
- Barbershop / beauty salon — preset (service item type); appointments, commission deferred.
- Pharmacy/wholesale — explicitly out of MVP presets (regulatory/credit complexity).

## Rule
A preset is configuration, not a separate product. New verticals reuse the core.
