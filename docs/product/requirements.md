# Requirements (Phase 1 classification)

Legend: MVP / Next / Future / Industry-specific / Unconfirmed.

## MVP
Auth + roles, org/branch/register, catalog (products, variants, barcodes,
units, cost/price, VAT flag), movement-ledger inventory, online-only checkout
(search, scan, cart, discounts gated, server-computed VAT, cash/manual-card/
wallet, split tender, hold×1, idempotency, receipt), shifts + cash control,
returns against original sale, purchasing lite (supplier, PO, receive),
8 canned reports, audit trail.

## Next
Customer profiles + store credit, price lists, promotions, stock counts,
purchase returns, PWA installability, barcode labels.

## Future
Subscriptions/billing, loyalty, gift cards, reservations, accounting
integration, KDS, table maps, appointments, scale integration, true offline
sync, ZATCA e-invoicing / Egypt e-receipt certification.

## Industry-specific (deferred)
Recipes, modifiers/combos/split-bill/tips, scale/weighted, waste tracking,
batch/lot traceability. Pharmacy suitability is NOT claimed from generic
batch/expiry alone.

## Unconfirmed
Wholesale credit terms, full Arabic RTL QA in MVP vs Next.
